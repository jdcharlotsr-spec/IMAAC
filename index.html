About UsOur Mission

Our Mission

To unite Apostles, Prophets, Evangelists, Pastors and Teachers in Kingdom purpose, equipping the saints and advancing the Gospel worldwide.

Our Vision

To see the Body of Christ mature in unity, walking in apostolic order and fulfilling the Great Commission in power and demonstration.

Our Purpose

To operate in Five Fold ministry, empowered by the Holy Ghost, bringing transformation to individuals, communities and nations.

Our Motto

Until we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God. Ephesians 4:13.

Our Presiding Apostle

Apostle Develron D. Walker

Presiding Apostle & Chief Overseer

A man called by God, anointed for such a time as this, to establish, equip and empower leaders and ministries for Kingdom advancement. Apostle Walker carries a burden for unity, apostolic order and the manifestation of the power of the Holy Ghost in this generation.

With a deep passion for souls and a commitment to biblical truth, Apostle Walker operates in the Five Fold Ministry with a global vision. He believes in raising leaders, building strong ministries, and transforming communities through the power of the Gospel.

Develron D. Walker emerges from humble beginnings, marked by early achievements. Born on February 3, 1984, to Gary and the late Deborah Walker, he is the sixth child among seven siblings.

His educational journey began in the Aldine Independent School District in Houston, Texas. He later pursued ministerial training at World Harvest Bible College in Columbus, Ohio, obtaining a Diploma of Arts in Pastoral Ministries and Church Administration.

As Presiding Apostle and Chief Overseer of IMAAC, Apostle Walker carries an apostolic mantle to establish order in the house of God, strengthen the Five Fold offices, develop leaders, and call the Church into unity, integrity, and Kingdom purpose.
